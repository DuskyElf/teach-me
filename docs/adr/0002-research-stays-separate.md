# literature-review skill remains separate

Research (literature review, tension surfacing, source quality scoring) stays as its own skill. Teach-me does not create research files or do independent research mid-session.

Teach-me builds curriculum from the research file's summary, using its tension map and source links. When the curriculum is exhausted, it loads additional papers from the research file's source list — not from scratch.

Reason: Research and tutoring are different cognitive workflows. Research casts a wide net, scores sources, surfaces contradictions. Tutoring probes understanding through dialogue. Combining them would bloat the tutoring experience or shallow out the research.

Reverting this would mean merging research into teach-me as a subroutine — possible, but changes the character of both skills.
